# Session Changelog: Phase 3 - Gameplay Systems & AI Evolution

This session focused on transforming the engine from a driving sandbox into a functional arcade racer with dynamic track generation, competitive AI, and robust physics.

## 1. Core Gameplay Systems
* **Dynamic Checkpoint & Lap System**: Implemented a sequence-based checkpoint system. Crossing the final checkpoint (finish line) increments the lap counter and calculates/saves best lap times.
* **Smart Respawn (R Key)**: Replaced the static reset with a "Smart Respawn" that returns the car to the last passed checkpoint, oriented toward the next goal with zero velocity.
* **OOB & Fall Recovery**: Integrated automatic respawn logic for both Player and AI if they fall below the track height or leave the track mesh boundaries.
* **Real-time Ranking HUD**: Added a live leaderboard to the HUD that compares the Player's lap/checkpoint progress against AI competitors to determine race position (e.g., "Position: 1/4").
* **Replay System (P Key)**: Implemented a rolling 10,000-frame buffer that records car state. Pressing `P` toggles a playback mode to review the last minute of racing.

## 2. Dynamic Track & Pathfinding
* **Road Spine Extraction**: Developed `extractPathFromRoadMesh` to automatically generate racing lines from `track_road.ply`. 
* **Center-Line Heuristic**: Refined path extraction to find the "spine" of the road by averaging nearby triangle centers, ensuring AI and checkpoints are perfectly centered regardless of track width or complexity.
* **Visual Guidance**: Added a "Retro Beam" visualization—a large, translucent vertical pillar—at the current target checkpoint to guide the player through the course.

## 3. Advanced AI Intelligence
* **Racing Grid**: Implemented a 3-car AI grid with staggered spawning offsets.
* **Surface-Aware AI**: AI cars now query the track surface normal every frame, aligning their orientation to hills and banked turns identical to the player's physics.
* **Barrier Awareness**: AI cars now detect and react to `track_barrier.ply` using ray-casted collision detection.
* **Steering Fixes**: Resolved a critical Radian vs. Degree unit mismatch in the AI steering logic, resulting in responsive and accurate pathfollowing.
* **Stuck Recovery**: AI cars now employ velocity reflection when hitting walls and have a "jitter" factor to help them bounce back into the race line.

## 4. Physics & Collision Refinement
* **Hidden Wall Fix**: Implemented a normal-based filter for ground detection (`y > 0.5`). This ensures the car drives on the road surface and ignores vertical "thickness" faces or curbs that previously acted as invisible walls.
* **Outward-Only Collision Rays**: Refined barrier detection to use specific ray directions for each car corner, preventing "stickiness" caused by rays hitting the inside of a wall the car is already overlapping.
* **Closest-Hit Resolution**: Refactored the physics solver to only process the single closest triangle hit per ray, preventing force accumulation and jitter.
* **Velocity Decoupling**: Fixed a bug where AI car collisions were interfering with the player's velocity, ensuring a clean and independent driving experience.
